Caesura · small screen The phone is a different plan, not a narrower one Desktop shows the trip and the day at once. A phone can only hold one, so the day rail becomes the spine: it is always at the top, it never collapses, and everything under it is the day you picked. Four views collapse to two — Plan and Map — because Day columns and Calendar are both answers to “show me more at once”, which a phone cannot do. Tags survive intact: they are the one thing that makes a small screen bearable, because they let you ask for less.
Plan Header, day rail, filters, day. The budget is one line you can tap, not a card. Stop cards drop the 92px time gutter — time moves inside, in mono, where it still reads as data.
