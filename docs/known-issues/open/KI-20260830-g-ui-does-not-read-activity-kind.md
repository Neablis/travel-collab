### KI-2026-08-30-g — The UI barely reads `kind`, so a stop that IS travel looks like any other stop
- **Severity:** cosmetic / design delta (nothing is wrong on screen; the app just knows something it does not use)
- **Area:** `apps/web/src/components/lenses/TimelineLens.tsx` (`Leg`), and more generally every surface that renders a stop — `packages/contracts/src/activity.ts` (`ActivityKind`), `apps/web/src/components/board/activityKind.ts`.
- **What is true today.** M18 gave a stop a real `kind` (`booked` / `hold` / `idea` / `transit` / `planned`), and exactly three things read it:
  1. `activityKind.ts` — the badge on a stop card (`transit` shows **Travel**), and the picker.
  2. `needsBooking.ts:38` — `transit` is excluded from `N to book`.
  3. `conflicts.ts:179` — a `transit` stop between two stops IN TIME excuses an impossible-geography conflict. Its own comment is the clearest statement of intent in the repo: *"M18 added `ActivityKind: 'transit'` precisely so a surface could tell that a stop IS travel rather than a place you sit in."* On the Japan demo this removed 10 false conflicts.
- **The gap, as raised by Mitchell (2026-08-30), looking at the Timeline.** The leg between two stops reads *"30 m until next stop"*. `Leg({ prevEnd, nextStart })` takes **only the two times** — no activity, no `kind` — so it is pure arithmetic on a gap. A `transit` stop sitting between them is rendered as an ordinary stop with an ordinary gap either side, and the fact that the middle one is the *moving* is expressed nowhere in that column. The designs treat movement as a distinct thing; the UI does not.
- **This entry deliberately prescribes nothing.** Mitchell's framing: *"we should be more aware of the activity kind and modify the ui based on that knowledge."* The point is that `kind` is a fact the app already has and mostly ignores, and the Timeline leg is only the instance that happened to be noticed. Whoever picks this up should look at every surface that renders a stop and decide, per surface, what `kind` should change — not implement a specific leg treatment because this entry described one.
- **Two pieces of history that matter before anyone starts:**
  - `TimelineLens`'s `Leg` comment records that it *used* to do more: it appended a straight-line "km direct" distance and warned past a 30-minute gap, and **both were deliberately removed** (Phase 8, Task 8.1) because *"there is no real travel-time/distance data source reachable from the UI"*. Any movement treatment that implies a real duration or distance re-opens that, and the honest-data objection still stands.
  - `M18-stop-kind.md` records that SPEC §12's travel-day transit split **was built and then removed at the M18 gate**: it fired on one of seven travel days and got that one wrong, because every stop on a travel day carried the day's DESTINATION city. That was KI-59 — **now fixed** — so the specific data defect that sank the first attempt is gone. That does not make the design decision automatic, but it removes the reason it failed last time.
- **The one movement cue that does ship** is the day header's `Tokyo → Nikkō` transition, and it is derived from **city** (`chip.transitionTo`), not from `kind` — which is why KI-59 mattered to it and why it is easy to mistake for kind-awareness.
- **Raised by:** Mitchell, 2026-08-30, asking whether the Timeline's "30 m until next stop" indicator uses the travel kind as the designs show. It does not.
