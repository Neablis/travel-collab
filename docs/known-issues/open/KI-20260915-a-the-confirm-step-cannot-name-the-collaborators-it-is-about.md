### KI-2026-09-15-a — the `plans` confirm step cannot name the collaborators a plan change is about

- **Severity:** copy quality on a paying surface — no correctness impact. The sentence is true and general where the design's is true and specific.
- **Area:** `apps/web/src/components/plans/PlansScreen.tsx` — `gainSentence` and `cancelSentence`, the *What changes* column of SPEC §29's confirm step.
- **What is wrong:** §29 asks for *what changes* to be stated *"in the product's own terms and **with the real collaborator names**"*, and gives the example **"Mei, Priya, Sam and Kenji go back to editing Japan — no re-invites"**. The build says *"everyone else on your trips goes back to reading"*. The design's version is the stronger copy by a distance: it names the people and the trip, which is what makes a $19 charge feel like a thing rather than a tier.
- **Why not fixed there:** the names are not this screen's to know. `/api/account/plan` answers what an ACCOUNT holds; who is on its trips is Access & Membership's, and reaching for it would either put a trip query behind an account endpoint or have the plans route call a second, trip-scoped one. Both are the boundary ADR-045 rule 5 and AGENTS.md's module map draw, for a sentence. Naming the gap beat inventing a placeholder list or quietly shipping a weaker line as if it were the designed one.
- **Scope:** an account-scoped read that answers *"who would be capped if this account stopped holding `trip.collaborators`"* — distinct people across the account's trips, by display name, bounded and ordered. That is a real question the collaboration gate already answers per trip (`server/access/collaborationGate.ts`), so the work is an aggregate over it plus a wire field on `AccountPlanView`, not a new mechanism. Worth doing when somebody is next in that module; it is not worth a milestone of its own.
- **Cross-reference:** `.design-sync/handoff/SPEC.md` §29, `docs/milestones/M21-subscriptions-and-billing.md` (link 5 and *What was built*), ADR-047.
