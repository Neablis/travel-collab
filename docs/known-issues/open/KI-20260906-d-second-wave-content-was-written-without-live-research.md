### KI-2026-09-06-d — eight content bundles were written without live web access, so their prices and opening rules are model knowledge rather than checked facts

- **Severity:** content accuracy, bounded. Nothing is broken; the risk is a seeded playbook stating a wrong ticket price or opening time as if it were verified. Every one of these days is flagged `authorKind: "ai"` in the database and carries the "AI starter" badge, so no reader is told a person checked it.
- **Area:** `content/playbooks/` — `australia-newzealand`, `britain-ireland`, `finger-lakes-upstate-ny`, `france-cities`, `italy-cities`, `korea-taiwan-hongkong`, `turkey-balkans` (60 days), plus **half** of `california-coast-and-wine` (the three San Francisco days and the Napa day). Each of those files now opens its `bundle.sources` with a `PROVENANCE:` line saying so.
- **What happened:** the first eleven bundles (88 days) and the four demo trips were researched against live pages. The second and third waves ran after the session's WebSearch budget was spent (200/200) and while the egress proxy was refusing every outbound fetch — `EGRESS_BLOCKED` on official park, museum and operator domains, and on Wikipedia. Three of those agents reported it explicitly; five were killed by a session rate limit mid-write and two of their files had to be recovered from truncated JSON.
- **Why the files were kept rather than deleted:** they are seed content for a demo library, and "plausible, well-shaped, and labelled as generated" is the bar that content has to clear. The venues are real places, the day shapes are sound, and the schema and `lintBundle` checks they pass are about structure, which is unaffected. What they cannot claim is that a number in them was read off a page this year.
- **What the `sources` URLs are now:** canonical references for the facts asserted, explicitly NOT pages that were fetched. The `PROVENANCE:` line in each file says exactly that, so nobody reads the list as a citation trail.
- **The fix:** a verification pass, one bundle at a time, with live search — confirm ticket prices, opening days and seasonal closures, correct what has moved, and replace the `PROVENANCE:` line with the real source list. `pnpm content:verify` will not catch any of this: it checks structure and content rules, not whether a price is true. That is the point of this entry.
- **Do not treat a `PROVENANCE:`-marked bundle as a reference for a real trip** until that pass has happened.
- **Cross-reference:** ADR-041 (the format and why the content exists), **KI-2026-09-06-c** (the same content's missing coordinates, deliberate and for a related reason — an unverifiable fact is better omitted than asserted).
- **First noted:** 2026-09-06, on the branch that added the content.
