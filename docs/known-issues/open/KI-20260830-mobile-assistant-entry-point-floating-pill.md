### KI-2026-08-30 — The mobile assistant's entry point is a floating pill launcher, which SPEC §13.5 rules out outright
- **Severity:** cosmetic — but it is a *stated spec violation* rather than an accident, which is why it is filed rather than left in a fixed entry's footnote.
- **Area:** `apps/web/src/components/assistant/AssistantRail.tsx` (the launcher), `apps/web/src/app/globals.css` (`.assistant-rail`), `SPEC.md` §9/§10/§13.5.
- **What is wrong:** below 768px the rail itself is now correct — KI-84 made it a full-screen `position: fixed` surface, walked in a browser at 411×852 and 375px. The way you *open* it is not. It is still a pill that floats over the plan, and `SPEC.md` §13.5 forbids exactly that, in terms: *"Nothing floats over data. No floating action button."*
- **This was off-spec before KI-84's fix and is off-spec after it.** KI-84 changed what happens once the rail opens; it did not touch the launcher, and said so. Nothing regressed here — the violation is unchanged and pre-existing.
- **Why not fixed here:** redesigning the mobile entry point is a design decision, not a defect fix. It needs §10's "mobile is retrieval, not planning" framing plus a genuine phone surface, and neither exists yet. SPEC's two other assistant presentations — the 56px bubble and the 364×476 floating card (§9) — are still M16-deferred per Mitchell's 2026-08-25 call, and are the more likely long-term answer to the launcher question than anything bolted onto a full-screen rail.
- **Why it is its own entry:** it was the reason KI-84 sat in `## Open` marked "MOSTLY RESOLVED" while the defect it was actually filed for was fixed and proven. A closed defect held open by an unrelated design question is invisible work on both counts — the fix looks unfinished and the design question looks like a footnote.
