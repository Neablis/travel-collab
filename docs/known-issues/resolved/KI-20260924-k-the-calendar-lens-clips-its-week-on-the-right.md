### KI-2026-09-24-k — the Calendar lens clips its week on the right below desktop width — RESOLVED

- **Severity:** cosmetic/usability — the hidden days are reachable by scrolling sideways, but nothing says so.
- **Area:** `apps/web/src/components/lenses/CalendarLens.tsx`.
- **Symptom / What happens:** measured 2026-09-24: at 820px about 5.5 of 7 weekday columns show and Fri/Sat need a sideways scroll; at 1024px Saturday is clipped; at 390px ~2.5 columns show (a phone reaches Calendar only by URL, which matches SPEC §10's two-views rule).
- **Why not fixed here:** out of the stop-editor fix's scope; wants a narrow-width Calendar decision (fewer columns, week paging, or a visible scroll affordance).
- **Cross-reference:** screenshot `09-calendar-820.png` from the 2026-09-24 check.
- **First noted:** 2026-09-24, mobile check.
- **Resolved 2026-09-25** (overnight KI sweep). The week still scrolls sideways — its 144px column floor makes it 1014px wide — but it now says so. `CalendarLens.tsx`'s new `MonthBlock` measures which weekday header cells are not wholly inside the month's scroller and, while any are, draws a 32px `from-surface` fade on that edge and puts a ghost button in the month header naming them (`Fri–Sat ›`, aria "Scroll to Fri–Sat") that scrolls them in; after scrolling, the days pushed off the left get their own `‹ Sun–Mon` button. Nothing appears where the week fits (≥1054px of content). Reproduced first against `/demo?view=Calendar` in Chromium: at 820px the scroller ended at x=800 with Fri at 750–894 and Sat at 895–1039, at 1024px it ended at 1004 with Sat at 895–1039, and no element on the page named either day; after the fix the same measurement shows a visible `Fri–Sat` control at 820px, `Sat` at 1024px, and none at 1280px. Regression test: `e2e/responsive.spec.ts` "a Calendar week that runs off the side names the hidden days, and the control reaches them" (narrow project) — at 820 and 1024px it reads the hidden weekdays from real geometry and requires the control naming them; at 820px it clicks it and requires Saturday fully in and the left-hand control to appear; at 1100px it requires no control. With the right-side measurement disabled it fails `expect(locator).toHaveCount(expected) … getByTestId('calendar-month').first().getByTestId('calendar-week-scroll') Expected: 1 Received: 0`. Checks: `pnpm --filter web typecheck`, eslint on both changed files, `CalendarLens.test.tsx` (34 passed), and the spec in `test:e2e:ci-like`.
- **Decision (2026-09-25 overnight sweep):** a visible scroll affordance (edge fade + a header control naming the hidden weekdays), not fewer columns and not week paging. Fewer/narrower columns was rejected because the 144px floor is measured from the widest line a cell renders — at 1024px it would need ~139px and at 820px ~111px, so something in every cell would truncate, the defect the floor was introduced to fix. Week paging was rejected because SPEC §4 lays a month out as one stacked block of whole weeks with "no month paging", and paging within a week breaks the week shape the view exists to show. A fade alone was rejected as too quiet — the scrollbar that already existed sits a screen below the cut, and a mouse wheel scrolls down, not sideways — so the control is what makes the hidden days both announced and reachable. Phone (390px) inherits the same control; SPEC §10 keeps Calendar off the phone's tabs, so no phone-specific layout was added.
