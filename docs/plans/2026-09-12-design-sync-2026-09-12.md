# Pulling in the 2026-09-12 design handoff

Written 2026-09-12 on `claude/lucid-bohr-m2v9oo`, from the bundle committed as
`4e1470b` ("Design sync handoff"). It replaces the 2026-09-05 bundle and is, by
its own README, *"the largest since the bundle started"*.

**This is a plan, not a milestone file and not a scope decision.** Where the work
goes is Mitchell's call (`AGENTS.md`, "Milestone discipline": *do not build ahead
of the current milestone*), and the current milestone is **M9, exit gate 0/10**.
Nothing below is started. What this file does is state, with real file paths and
real numbers, what each design change costs against the code as it stands, in
what order the pieces unlock each other, and the three questions that have to be
answered before two of the tranches can be built at all.

`.design-sync/handoff/DRIFT.md` is the design side's own reconciliation and it is
good; this is the build side of the same ledger, and it disagrees with the
handoff in four places, each marked **⚠ conflict** below.

## What the handoff changed

Five new spec sections, all dated 2026-09-12:

| § | Change | The user's framing |
|---|---|---|
| §24 | Trip tabs become **Overview · Plan · Calendar · Map**; Timeline deleted; Calendar/Map scopes swapped | — |
| §25 | Overview **is** the trip's undeletable notebook page | — |
| §26 | **No widget control in the document flow** — settings move to the desktop right column and the phone sheet | "Widget Experience" |
| §27 | Trip lifecycle (optimistic delete + undo), read-only as one mode with two entrances | — |
| §28 | New mark (◎ → ‖), **Ledger becomes the default look**, the phone front door, day columns sort by start time | "Rethemed the entire website" + "Homepage mobile experience" |

The "other small ones" are real but genuinely small: the collapsed assistant
becomes a 92×44 `Ask` bar, the `vibe` prop is deleted, and §28 answers the
build's open question about day-column sort order (yes, by start time).

## The honest starting line

The handoff is closer to the build than its size suggests, because three of the
five sections land on seams that already exist:

- **§27 read-only is already the build's model.** `DemoTripScreen.tsx:19-36` and
  ADR-031 already say what §27 says: `/demo` mounts *the same provider stack and
  the same `TripBoardScreen`* as `(app)/trips/[tripId]/page.tsx`, and read-only
  comes from the visitor being a `viewer` at the access seam, not from a second
  screen. The design arrived independently at the architecture that shipped.
- **§27's two verbs exist as commands.** `RestoreTrip` (`TripHeader.tsx:121`),
  `DeleteTrip` and `duplicateTrip` (`SettingsSheet.tsx:169,180`). `toast.tsx` is
  a built primitive. What is missing is the per-card `···` popover, not the
  machinery under it.
- **§26 has both of its destinations already built.** The desktop right column is
  `WidgetInsert` (`PageScreen.tsx:564`) and the phone bind sheet is
  `WidgetChrome.tsx`'s phone branch over `widgetBind.tsx`'s option lists. §26 is
  a relocation of 233 lines, not a new subsystem.
- **A seeded "Trip Overview" page already exists** —
  `packages/pages/src/templates.ts:124`, `seedIntoNewTrips: true`. §25 needs it to
  become undeletable, widget-bearing and eagerly created, but the seed slot is
  there.

Against that, two sections are new surface area with nothing under them: the look
layer (§28) does not exist in any form, and the phone front door (§28) has no
responsive handling at all — `LandingScreen.tsx` (265 lines) contains no
breakpoint, no `useIsPhone`, and no media query.

## ⚠ Four places the handoff and the build disagree

These are the reason this is a plan and not a task list. Three need Mitchell.

### 1. §24 deletes the phone's only editing surface — **blocking**

`PhoneTabBar.tsx:141` points the phone's **Plan** tab at
`?lens=Schedule&view=Timeline` — the phone's Plan *is* the Timeline lens.
`usePhoneTwoViews` (`TripViewTabs.tsx:73-86`) exists for the sole purpose of
normalising a bare `/trips/<id>` to Timeline on a phone, on SPEC §10's grounds:

> **Two views, not four.** Day columns and Calendar exist to show *density*,
> which a phone cannot show honestly.

§24 deletes Timeline and makes Plan (day columns) *"the only surface that
edits"*, on both surfaces. §10 and §22 are carried forward in this same bundle,
unchanged. So the bundle simultaneously says the phone's editing surface is day
columns and that a phone cannot render day columns honestly. Either §10 is
superseded and the phone gets day columns, or Timeline survives as the phone's
Plan and only the desktop tab is deleted. **The handoff does not say which, and
tranche 3 cannot start until it does.**

### 2. `w-open` and `w-people` are not registry additions — they are new data

§25: *"A build must add `w-open` to the registry"*, and names *"the existing
`w-people`"*. Neither exists, and the build's registry cannot take them as-is.
`packages/pages/src/registry.ts:12` is explicit (ADR-039 decision 1):

> **Twelve primitives, and nothing else.**

The twelve are `cost, count, dates, hours, city, attribute, day.detail,
city.detail, day.rows, city.rows, stop.rows, cost.rows`. Named widgets like
`w-open` are *presets* — `(primitive, params, title, keywords)` rows — and a
preset can only express what a primitive already resolves.

- **`w-open` ("What needs you" — overlaps, empty days, parked ideas)** is not a
  projection any of the twelve produce. Overlaps live in `overlapData.ts` and the
  conflict engine; parked ideas are the unscheduled rack. It is a **thirteenth
  primitive**, which means amending ADR-039's decision 1 — a deliberate,
  documented boundary, not an oversight to route around.
- **`w-people` ("Who's in") has no data at all.** There is no traveller or person
  field anywhere in `packages/contracts` — the only occurrence is a *count* in a
  share fixture (`share.ts:36`, `"4 travellers"`). The nearest real data is M11's
  `trip_memberships`, which is collaborators, not travellers, and which
  `packages/pages` may not reach: a resolver reads `TripDetail`, and membership
  is an access-seam concern (`server/access/members.ts`).

So §25's "one registry addition owed" is, in build terms, **one ADR amendment,
one new primitive, and one unanswered question about whose faces appear in "Who's
in".**

### 3. Ledger's city rule assumes a hue ramp the build does not have

§28: city-coded things get *"a 3px solid rule in its own city colour"* with
*"tint chroma and the solid both bumped for this look only"*, and DRIFT
build-check 2 speaks of an oklch hue ramp with a 35° minimum gap.

The build does not colour cities from a hue ramp. `lib/dayAccent.ts:1` assigns
each city one of **five semantic families** — `brand, info, success, warning,
danger` — and `cityAccents.ts` exists specifically so the board, the home hero
and notebook widgets cannot disagree about the assignment
(`dayAccentConsistency.property.test.ts` pins it). There is no chroma to bump:
Ledger's city rule becomes *"override `--color-<family>-tint` per look, and draw
the rule with `--color-<family>-ink`"*. That is a smaller job than the spec text
implies, and it is a different one. Worth confirming the intent survives the
translation before building it, because "carries harder in Ledger" is a
perceptual claim about a ramp that does not exist here.

### 4. The Overview tab can render nothing, on two paths

§25 says *"every trip is created with one notebook page it cannot delete"*. The
build seeds lazily instead: `instantiateDefaults` is called from `listPages`
(`server/pages.ts:5`), so **a trip whose Notebook index has never been opened has
no pages at all** — its Overview tab would render an empty document. And the demo
trip is seeded *in memory only*, deliberately: seeding it wrote database rows for
a visitor with no session, which was KI-2026-09-05-d and ADR-031's *"the only
database work in the whole demo is the clone"*.

So §25 needs the seed to move to trip creation for real trips **and** to keep the
in-memory fold for the demo — two paths, one of which is a documented invariant
that a straightforward "seed on create" would break.

## The work, in tranches

Dependency-ordered. Tranches 1, 2, 6 and 7 are independent of each other and of
the blocking question; 3 → 4 → 5 is a chain.

### T1 — The mark, and the collapsed assistant (§28) — small

Six live sites render `◎`: `TripBoardScreen.tsx:1040`, `AskPill.tsx:57`,
`AssistantRail.tsx:412`, `AssistantBubble.tsx:46`, `SaveLight.tsx:99`,
`FrontDoorHeader.tsx:15`. The glyph becomes `‖` in a 32px tile, 4px radius, 18px
strokes; `SaveLight` keeps the pulse (§11).

`AssistantBubble` is the one that is not a find-and-replace: today it is a 56px
round `size-14` button carrying the mark — *exactly* the treatment §28 records as
tried and rejected (*"read as a logo, not a control"*). It becomes a 92×44 bar
reading **Ask**. §28 warns that `asstSize(false)` must match the markup because
clamping and open/close anchoring both read it; the build's equivalent is
whatever the drag/clamp path measures, and that has to move with it.

`AssistantRail.test.tsx:297` asserts the glyph and will fail — correctly.

### T2 — The look layer, and Ledger as default (§28) — the retheme

The app has no theming of any kind. What it does have is the right shape for one:
`globals.css`'s `@theme` block (line 6) is the single source of truth, `--color-*:
initial` wipes Tailwind's palette so nothing can reference an off-system colour,
and radius, shadow and font are all tokens too (`--radius-sm…full` at :82,
`--shadow-raised/overlay/float/lifted` at :89, `--font-display/sans/mono` at
:107). Every one of Ledger's structural rules is therefore a **token override**,
not a markup change:

| Ledger says | Build does |
|---|---|
| square every corner but the truly round ones | override `--radius-sm…2xl` → `0`; leave `--radius-full` |
| drop card shadows for hairline rules | override `--shadow-raised` → `none`, add a hairline border rule |
| headings in mono | `--font-display: var(--font-next-mono)` |
| city colour carries harder | per-family tint overrides + a 3px `border-left` (see conflict 3) |

So the retheme is: a `[data-look="…"]` block per look in `globals.css`, the
attribute written on the shell, four looks (`ledger, paper, nightdesk, airmail`)
with **ledger as the default**, and a picker. Two decisions ride on it —

- **Is `look` a user preference or a constant?** The design file carries it as a
  prop with four options, which says preference; the build's preference row is
  `UserPreferences` (`contracts/identity.ts:38`, three fields today) and adding a
  fourth is **a migration**. §28's parenthetical — *"`look`, not `theme`, because
  the old key holds a stale stored value"* — is about the design file's own
  storage and does not bind us, but it does imply a stored value.
- **`vibe` is already absent** from the build. Nothing to delete; noted so it is
  not re-raised.

The honest cost is not the token blocks — those are an afternoon. It is that
1,080 lines of `globals.css` contain literals that predate any look (line 554's
*"18px top radius and the upward shadow are design literals with no token"* is
the self-documented example), and each one is a thing Ledger cannot square.

### T3 — Four tabs (§24) — **blocked on conflict 1**

The URL model changes shape. Today: `?lens=Board|Map|Schedule` ×
`?view=Timeline|Calendar` (`LensRouter.tsx:13-16`). §24 wants four peer views with
`dayScope = view === 'columns' || view === 'map'`.

- `TimelineLens.tsx` (1,014 lines) and its test (860) are **deleted**, not ported
  — §24 is explicit. `ScheduleLens.tsx` (39 lines) collapses with them.
- `LensRouter`, `TripViewTabs` and `TripViewTabs.test.tsx:70` (which asserts the
  four old labels) are rewritten. Old `?lens=` URLs must keep resolving: the
  m10-growth e2e spec loads `?lens=Board` at 411px *on purpose*, and
  `PhoneTabBar` builds `?lens=` hrefs.
- **Calendar and Map swap scopes.** Clicking a Calendar cell must *select* a day
  without navigating into it — today Calendar is a `Schedule` view and Map clears
  focus.
- **Focus survives tab switches**, one `focus` per trip across all four
  (`FocusProvider.tsx`, 550 lines, already the single holder — DRIFT build-check 3
  warns derivation stays per-surface).
- `lensAcceptsDrops.ts` is renamed along with Board→Plan. Its rule does not
  change, and its header comment is explicit that it must stay a question about
  drop targets rather than a lens whitelist.
- **The day chip rail goes on all four tabs**, including the two trip-scope ones
  — §24 flags this as a deliberate, argued exception to rule 2.

### T4 — Widget settings out of the document flow (§26)

The rule is absolute and it is the one that supersedes the most: *"The document
reads identically in both modes. No widget control is ever in the document
flow."*

- `WidgetChrome.tsx` (233 lines) stops rendering inline. Its desktop half moves
  into `WidgetInsert`'s column as a **second state** — rail when nothing is
  selected, settings when something is, both 320px so switching does not reflow.
  Its phone half becomes the inspector, gaining the tag filters, Wording and
  Remove that were inline.
- `MacroNodeView.tsx` gains the dashed outline and the 58×20 `▸` handle, both
  **outside the text measure** so prose does not move.
- The repeater's "Prints N stops" line, its tag chips and its "Edit the wording"
  link come out of the page in both modes.
- `widgetBind.tsx`'s `inline` variant (`:260`) loses its only caller.
- §26 answers ADR-037 d6 and **KI-2026-09-05-c** (*"the widget editor is inline
  with its value"*) — that KI closes with this tranche.
- The tradeoff to land deliberately: the measure changes once when edit mode
  opens, chosen over a permanent empty 320px gutter.

Do this **before** T5. Building the Overview page against a chrome row that is
being deleted is throwaway work.

### T5 — Overview is a notebook page (§25) — **blocked on conflict 2**

Given T3's tab and T4's settings model:

- Seed the page at trip creation, keeping the demo's in-memory fold (conflict 4).
- Make it undeletable *with a stated refusal*, not a hidden control — the delete
  path is `server/pages.ts`'s `deletePage` plus `pages-guard.ts`.
- It appears in the Notebook index as "Overview / Comes with the trip".
- The tab renders through the same resolver path the Notebook route uses; its one
  button opens the page in the Notebook. **Editing never happens on the tab.**
- `w-open` and `w-people` per conflict 2.
- Accept what §25 accepts: because it is a real page, a user can empty it.

### T6 — Trip lifecycle and read-only (§27) — small

- A per-card `···` popover on `TripCard.tsx` with **Duplicate** and **Delete**,
  one level deep (rule 3). Both verbs already exist as commands; today they live
  in `SettingsSheet` *inside* the trip.
- Optimistic delete: the card goes on the click, a toast carries a single
  **Undo** → `RestoreTrip`. 6s with an action, 2.4s without.
- Duplicate lands a "(copy)" card with **dates and travellers cleared**, no
  confirm dialog.
- A shared trip offers **Leave this trip** instead of Delete.
- Read-only: audit that the gated controls are **absent, not disabled** — Add
  stop, per-day add, the Keep pennant, Edit in Notebook, the unscheduled drawer —
  and that `propose` and both proposal **Keep** handlers refuse at source. `Ask`
  stays available and its context line reads *"Reading <trip> · answers only"*.

The architecture is already right (ADR-031); this is an audit plus a popover.

### T7 — The phone front door (§28) — independent

`LandingScreen.tsx` has no responsive handling whatsoever, so this is additive: a
pinned sequence where the map and headline hold still while four claims pass
underneath, then the map clears and the CTA arrives on empty paper.

Three build notes come with it, all of which read like they were paid for once:

1. Progress is **scroll position inside the pinned block**, not time.
2. The rest state must be **authored into the markup** (first chunk visible, rest
   at zero) or a cold load stacks all four.
3. **Do not wrap the scroll work in `requestAnimationFrame`** — in a throttled or
   hidden frame the callback never runs and the "already scheduled" guard latches
   forever, silently killing the effect. Synchronous transform/opacity writes
   only.

Note that `LandingHeroArt.tsx` (432 lines) keeps its `pointer-events: none`
decorative layers; DRIFT §2 says the same trap exists in any real implementation.

## Traps

- **`.design-sync/**` is a build input, not prose** (`AGENTS.md:284`). A branch
  touching it is Tier 2 verification, not the zero-check prose tier. `4e1470b`
  already put code-adjacent files on this branch, so **this branch is not
  prose-only** and the full suite applies per CLAUDE.md rule 4.
- **KI-49 bounds what T3 may claim.** Map tiles have never been confirmed to
  paint in any environment; a blank WebGL canvas is not a pass. Any browser walk
  of the rescoped Map tab has to say so.
- **DRIFT build-check 2 still binds**, and it is the reason conflict 3 matters:
  MapLibre parses CSS Color 3 only and falls back to black on `oklch()`, while
  `getComputedStyle` and canvas `fillStyle` both *preserve* it verbatim and look
  like a fix. Any look-dependent colour that reaches a map paint property must be
  converted arithmetically.
- **Do not port Timeline's markup** (§24) and do not re-express `lensAcceptsDrops`
  as a lens list (its own header says why).
- The handoff's **`Wireframes.dc.html` exploration is not a build instruction** —
  the README says so explicitly. Five competing planning models, nothing decided.

## What has to be answered before building

1. **Does the phone get day columns, or does Timeline survive as the phone's
   Plan?** (conflict 1) — blocks T3, and T5 behind it.
2. **Is `w-open` a thirteenth primitive, and does "Who's in" mean collaborators
   or travellers?** (conflict 2) — blocks T5. The first needs an ADR-039
   amendment; the second needs either a field that does not exist or a
   reinterpretation onto `trip_memberships`.
3. **Is `look` a stored user preference?** (T2) — if yes, it is a migration on
   `UserPreferences` and belongs with whatever milestone owns the retheme.
4. **Where does this go?** M9 is current at 0/10 and none of this is M9's. T4 and
   T5 are plainly M14's (which already *"owns the whole Notebook redesign"*); T1,
   T2 and T7 belong to no milestone that exists — M10 was the visual craft pass
   and its gate closed 2026-08-27. `TODO.md:585` already records the same gap
   for the phone — *"no milestone in this file owns the phone at all … placing
   the phone is a milestone-sized decision, not something to bolt onto whichever
   milestone touches a screen next"* — and the same sentence fits a retheme.

## What this plan does not cover

The handoff's carried sections — billing (§17), Playbooks (§15), the assistant on
the phone (§23), the widget framework itself (§21) — are unchanged by this pass
and already have homes. `DRIFT.md` §1's open items (D3, D6/KI-34, D10, D11) are
the design side's list and none of them moved on 2026-09-12; D11's two orphaned
`unplaced` wizard shells are still waiting on Mitchell, and DRIFT's own suggested
order puts that and the quota-window question ahead of everything here.
